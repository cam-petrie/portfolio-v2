Case 01 / Cognitive Talent Solutions

Cognitive Talent Analyzer™

An organizational network analysis platform that turns survey responses and collaboration data into maps, reports, and insights for workforce decisions.

01 / The problem

Make workplace relationships visible.

An org chart shows reporting lines. It can miss who people go to for advice, where teams collaborate, and who connects groups that rarely work together. Cognitive Talent Analyzer gives organizations a way to collect and explore that evidence.

I led development across survey setup, participant management, response tracking, and the dashboards and PDF reports teams use to review findings.

02 / Engineering decision

Give survey records and relationships different homes.

Survey campaigns need to track participants and responses. Network exploration needs to follow relationships between people. I used PostgreSQL for survey and participant records and Neo4j for the relationships explored in the network views.

Survey email delivery runs asynchronously, so it can proceed separately from campaign setup and response tracking. The backend also enforces tenant boundaries so organizations can work in the same product with separate data.

03 / What shipped

From survey launch to findings teams can use.

Teams can run surveys in five languages, explore networks in ActiveONA, and share results through dashboards and PDF reports. I also built AI-assisted onboarding and analysis with Cogni and contributed causal retention dashboards that help teams explore possible interventions.
